the Declaration for Global Peace

We, the unified voters of GLOBAL PEACE YES, declare that Global Peace is absolutely vital in the 21st Century for the human race to not only survive, but thrive in balance with all living beings. With this enduring commitment in mind, all GLOBAL PEACE YES verified votes support the establishment of Ministries & Departments of Peace in governments worldwide to help resolve conflict before it leads to violence. We understand that Global Peace ultimately comes from inner peace. Therefore, we pledge to practice being peace every day in rewarding service to the greater good and to promote peace education as fundamental to our world’s well being. Furthermore, we recognize we are blessed with the sacred gifts of Planet Earth and call for a regenerative way of life that does no harm to nature so future generations can benefit from nature’s gifts and our bountiful planet as we have. Finally, we seek to help build Economies of Peace so we “beat our swords into ploughshares” and bring forth a sustainable human renaissance and lasting golden age of nonviolence. Thank you for becoming the Peace Champions our world deserves and needs.
