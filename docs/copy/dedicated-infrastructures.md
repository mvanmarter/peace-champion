# Dedicated Infrastructures of Peace

## Government Ministries and Councils

### 1. Ethiopia – Ministry of Peace

A federal institution responsible for conflict prevention, conflict management, national reconciliation, unity, and cooperation between federal and regional governments.

- **Established:** 2018
- **Mandate revised:** 2021

### 2. Costa Rica – Ministry of Justice and Peace

Peace is explicitly integrated into justice, violence prevention, alternative dispute resolution, peace education, and civic coexistence.

Its **National System for the Promotion of Peace and Citizen Coexistence** coordinates several relevant programmes.

### 3. Solomon Islands – Ministry of Traditional Governance, Peace and Ecclesiastical Affairs

Originally established in 2002 as the **Ministry of National Unity, Reconciliation and Peace**. Its present form combines traditional governance, conflict mediation, reconciliation ceremonies, peace education, and psychosocial healing.

Particularly interesting for integrating indigenous leadership and customary approaches to conflict resolution.

### 4. Nepal – Ministry of Peace and Reconstruction

A historically important example. It was created to coordinate implementation of the **2006 Comprehensive Peace Agreement** following the civil war. Its **Peace Trust Fund** helped coordinate and finance peace-related programmes.

### 5. Ghana – National Peace Council

An institutionalized approach to mediation, dialogue, early warning, and conflict prevention.

Particularly interesting for its national and regional peace infrastructure.

## International Network

### Global Alliance for Ministries and Departments of Peace (GAMIP)

An international network of civil society organizations, peace advocates, and government representatives working to establish dedicated peace institutions around the world.

Its vision is to make peacebuilding a core responsibility of government, rather than something addressed only after violence and conflict occur.

**What GAMIP does:**

- Promotes national Ministries and Departments of Peace
- Connects peace movements in more than 30 countries
- Shares institutional models and peacebuilding experience
- Organizes international gatherings and campaigns
