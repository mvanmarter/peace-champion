“Admissions” stars Academy Award® nominee and Emmy Award winner James Cromwell. It has won over 30 international awards, has been translated into Spanish, German, Italian, Arabic, Hebrew, and Farsi, and has been broadcast to 80 million homes.

This deeply relevant modern parable is taught in the world-renowned curriculum of The Stanford University Forgiveness Project and has been screened in prestigious venues across the globe, including:

  • The Parliament of World Religions
  • The Jimmy Carter Presidential Library
  • The Rotary Global Peace Conference

“The Time Is Now,” the theme song in the end credits, was a finalist in the Global Peace Song Awards.

Short film

The Principle

The international reach and instant, 24-hour accessibility of today’s internet and social media platforms make modern cyberbullying far more destructive than traditional bullying.

This devastating plague of the digital age affects millions of people and is rapidly spreading. Increasing numbers of children, teenagers, and adults are being cyberbullied, both anonymously and by people they know, with often tragic consequences like suicide and extreme acts of gun violence.

In “The Principle,” an enlightened master played by 4-time Grammy Award nominee Mickey Guyton is called upon to moderate between two teenagers — a painfully misguided cyberbully and the tragic target of his abuse. In this life-changing confrontation, the master delivers transformational wisdom that alters the two young men’s destinies and is designed to help heal cyberbullying in the world at large.

Peace Champion App

A wise answer to life’s biggest questions.

  1. How do you develop the wisdom to remain peaceful, happy, productive, and strong no matter what comes your way in life?

To help you answer questions like these and turn life’s infinite challenges into endless growth and expansion, we warmly invite you to download our Peace Champion app and its catalytic catalog of peace practices.

Download your rejuvenating version here and begin your transformational journey to greater peace and joy and the enriching life you deserve.

Overview

The Peace Champion Mobile App provides a 1000-exercise Peace Education program — The Thousand-Petaled Lotus Training. This original, wisdom-based instruction is profound and multi-layered, addressing mind, body, spirit, and the crucial evolutionary importance of service. Presented in short, clever, thought-provoking installments, it takes only a minute a day to complete to receive its lasting positive impact. All of our wisdom offerings are made with love by human intelligence.

Upon completion of the transcendent training, users will earn a personalized Certificate of Achievement and shareable Peace Champion seal, which can be included in resumes, curricula vitae, and LinkedIn pages to establish them in the high-reward life of a Peace Champion. To celebrate and honor this evolution, 10 Peace Trees will be planted in the Peace Champion’s name, so they immediately contribute to making peace with Planet Earth.

User Profile

Peace Champions are on a lifelong journey of self-improvement, becoming stronger by turning life’s challenges into momentous growth. They understand that Global Peace begins with inner peace. Therefore, Peace Champions practice peace daily and, through their learning and service, build Muscles of Peace that help create a more rewarding life for themselves and a kinder, more loving world.

Similar to how athletes practice their sport to excel on the field of competition, Peace Champions practice the art of peacefulness to excel on the field of life. This is how they wisely support the world’s well-being and their own well-being at the same time.

The Thousand-Petaled Lotus Peace Mandala

Every petal leads to peace.

All Peace Champion app activities are tracked in a meaningful spiral on our app’s visually striking Thousand-Petaled Lotus Progress Mandala. The Lotus Mandala represents each Peace Champion’s exciting progress on the path to higher states of consciousness. This life-enhancing private journal keeps track of:

  • Inner growth
  • Peace and health practices
  • Acts of service
  • Wisdom offerings
  • Lessons learned
  • Personal reflections

This engaging virtual platform is designed to be an interactive “Inspiration Point” where Peace Champions can spend quality time each day and be motivated by all they are accomplishing on behalf of themselves and the world. It also provides an ongoing Petal Count to show how far each Peace Champion has risen toward the transformational goal of 1000 Golden Petals earned. Each Golden Petal is rewarded with corresponding Peace Points that are redeemable for our Mind Over Fabric positive-message apparel at our online store.
