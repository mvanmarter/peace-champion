Mission

Our mission is to support the evolution of human values, consciousness and capacities by strengthening global peace awareness and offering fun platforms for practicing peace together through art, culture, films, movement, technology, education and rewarding service to the greater good.

Strategy

In our crisis-filled modern world beset by so much division, conflict, and war, how do we unify and mobilize enough people to create the conditions for Global Peace to emerge?

Tipping point theory states that a new behavior must be adopted by 15-25% of the overall group before it spreads across the entire population and brings systemic change. According to this calculation then, if we get ONE BILLION people to vote for peace and then practice peace, we have passed the crucial threshold for this uplifting behavior to spread to the 8 billion plus members of our human family.

It all starts with voting YES for The Declaration for Global Peace and downloading our free Peace Champion app and its catalytic catalog of wisdom and peace practices. This is how we raise the collective vibration of humankind for the benefit of all living beings, one Peace Champion at a time. To deepen the impact of this movement and ground it firmly in the world, a Peace Tree will be planted for every vote to help humankind make peace with Planet Earth.

Vision

Peace Champion is a modern cultural and technological movement to help human beings across our globe embody peace and make peace a daily practice. This 50-year project (2025-2075) envisions a unified, compassionate and thriving world in which peace is the operating principle for individuals, communities, organizations and nations.

This co-created world will be founded on kindness, harmony and order across the inner, interpersonal, international and environmental levels. Our ultimate goal is to help bring forth a sustainable human renaissance and a lasting golden age of nonviolence for humankind.

As a California 501c3 nonprofit organization founded in 2013, our seminal program is for ONE BILLION people from every country and culture across the earth to cast a verified vote YES in support of our Declaration for Global Peace. This historic Declaration fully integrates and supports The Muscles of Peace Global Resolution in Support of Infrastructures for Peace in Governments Worldwide, a global peace campaign widely endorsed by Global Citizen Signatories from 192 nations and peacebuilding experts, civil society organizations, youth, key political figures, community leaders, and academia.

A huge step forward for this movement and for humankind was achieved in 2023 when the UN's "A New Agenda for Peace" under "Preventing Conflict and Violence and Sustaining Peace," made the official recommendation to all member states to "develop national prevention strategies and strengthen national infrastructures for peace."

Values

    Our organization is built upon kind gestures of peace and goodwill, like a Peace Dove carrying an olive branch to all members of our beloved global family.

  • Unity

    We are united with all our neighbors on Planet Earth and stand for the idea that humanity is our home team, and even in great numbers, we are all one.

  • Energy

    Just like the healing sun shining on all things equally, our organization endeavors to kindle warm, positive energy dedicated to service, in the heart of all human beings without exception.

  • Connection

    We honor the wisdom of the past and connect it to the present and the future to illuminate the best path through our challenging world, like the phases of the moon on the darkest nights of Winter.

  • Interdependence

    We understand the sacred power of humanity's interconnectivity and endeavor to take all human beings on the most rewarding user journey of all, from "me" to "we" and from "separation" to "unity."

    We trust that the cultivation of shared interests and common ground is the foundation of enduring peace, as exemplified by the healing communion of breaking bread.

Team

  • JV

    John Viscount

    Board Chair, Chief Visionary Officer, Lead Content Creator

    John is a peacebuilder, author, filmmaker, composer, and branding specialist. When John was in ninth grade, he started suffering from cluster migraine headaches - "one of the worst pains known to mankind" - according to the American Medical Association. Nicknamed "suicide headaches," they would come in large clusters of anywhere from 30 to 40 headaches at a time that hit all at once, one tortuous headache after the other.

    There was no known medical cure and after enduring the severe pain for 15 years, John eventually dove into spiritual teachings for help. Eventually, he healed the headaches by applying the wise guidance these luminous paths provide. This inspired John to want to share this wisdom so others could benefit as he had.

    To do this, he authored "Mind What Matters. A Pep Talk for Humanity" and "The Mind Supplement." He also creates global peace initiatives, peace-centered nonprofits, films, songs, wisdom campaigns, positive message apparel and peace branding campaigns. John also features this wisdom in the Thousand-Petaled Lotus Peace Training he created for our Peace Champion app.

    John's peace entertainment film "Admissions" addresses the Middle East crisis and the power of forgiveness to heal interpersonal and international conflicts. The film has won over 30 international awards and has been translated into multiple languages and broadcast to 80 million homes worldwide. During the global tour of the film, John met Scotty Bruer and they launched the non-profit, PeaceNow.com. Together they built numerous platforms for strengthening the global Culture of Peace, including their ONE BILLION Signatures for Peace Campaign.

    These platforms include a proposed United Nations Global Resolution called "The Muscles of Peace Global Resolution" for which John served as a lead drafter in collaboration with The Global Alliance for Ministries and Infrastructures for Peace. This international team of conflict resolution specialists included Peace Champion's brilliant Board Advisor, Iman Ibrahim. The resolution advocates for creating permanent governmental Infrastructures for Peace from the national to the local level and is widely endorsed by Global Citizen Signatories from 192 nations and peacebuilding experts, civil society organizations, youth, key political figures, community leaders, and academia.

    John's new film "The Principle", addresses cyberbullying with teachings on kindness and unity and stars 4-time Grammy nominee Mickey Guyton. It premiered at The Martin Luther King Jr Center for Nonviolent Social Change's "Be Love Day" celebration and now begins its global journey. John also has a full slate of peace entertainment film and music projects in development.
